# Licensing Constraints

This project must meet the following third-party licensing requirements:

- Record each dependency's name, version, license, and source.
- Preserve copyright and license notices when redistributing dependencies.
- Obtain legal approval before introducing dependencies with copyleft terms such as GPL or AGPL.
- Do not combine code with incompatible licenses without documented approval.
- Keep required attribution and license texts in the distribution package.
- Review dependency license changes during upgrades.
- Prefer permissive licenses such as MIT, BSD, or Apache-2.0 when practical.
- Escalate unknown, custom, missing, or non-commercial license terms before use.
